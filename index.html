How the numbers work

Ship base stats (Hull, Sail, and Gun HP, cannons per side, max crew, and forward speed) come straight from each hull's crafting values. Outfittings, Specialty Items, and Crew Supplies each add percentage bonuses on top — the calculator sums every bonus you've fitted and applies it to the base.

Crewmembers are modeled by profession, rank, and the pips you allocate to their bonus stats. Loyalty level increases (which slightly grow a crewmember's bonuses over time in-game) aren't factored in — this tool assumes a freshly-hired crewmember at base loyalty, so treat crew numbers as a floor rather than a ceiling.

Data sourced from the UO Outlands Wiki (Ships, Ship Upgrades, and Ship Crewmembers pages). This is an unofficial fan tool and isn't affiliated with UO Outlands or Electronic Arts.
