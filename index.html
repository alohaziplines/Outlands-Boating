How the numbers work

Every crafted ship rolls its own base stats — the "Roll %" column is the variance your specific ship rolled (shown in-game as "Base vs Average"). Enter that roll, and it stacks with every Outfitting, Specialty Item, and Crew Supply bonus you've fitted, applied straight to the ship type's underlying base. For most stats that's additive percentage on top of a real base value (Hull, Sail, Speed, Cannon Damage); a few stats — Crew Bravery, Crew Damage, Doubloons, Tidings, Effective Fishing, Spyglass — roll from zero, so their Final is just your roll plus your upgrades added directly. Cooldowns, Repair Cooldown, Cannon Reload Time, and Wake Scalar always reduce, so those fields auto-apply a minus sign for you.

Crewmembers are modeled by profession, rank, and the pips you allocate to their bonus stats. Loyalty level increases (which slightly grow a crewmember's bonuses over time in-game) aren't factored in — this tool assumes a freshly-hired crewmember at base loyalty, so treat crew numbers as a floor rather than a ceiling.
